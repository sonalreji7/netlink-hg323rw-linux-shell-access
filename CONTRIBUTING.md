# Contributing evidence

Document the exact hardware and firmware revision, acquisition method, and tool version. Label claims as observed, reported, reference-verified, or pending. Include an image digest when available; do not invent missing values.

Before committing text or images, remove passwords and hashes, MAC/BSSID addresses, serial numbers, SSIDs, private keys, GPON/LOID/PLOAM/PPPoE credentials, subscriber details, and private network identifiers. Review image pixels as well as metadata. Never commit raw material and rely on deleting it in a later commit.

Use inspection-only experiments on personally owned equipment in an isolated lab. No flash writes, account changes, or public service exposure are part of this guide.
